Link intervals.icu

  1. Make a free account at intervals.icu and connect your watch under Settings → Connections (Garmin, COROS, Suunto, Polar, Wahoo and more).
  2. On the same Settings page, scroll to Developer Settings and generate an API key, then copy it.
  3. Come back here, paste it below and tap Link.
