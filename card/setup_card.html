Next Mile TrackerYour miles, all year

  1. Plug the tracker into any USB phone charger.
  2. When the screen says Let's get connected, scan its QR code with your phone camera (or open WiFi settings and join NextMile-Tracker).
  3. A setup page opens. Tap Configure WiFi, pick your home WiFi and enter its password. 2.4 GHz WiFi only.
  4. Scan the new QR code on the screen, then tap Authorize in Strava.
  5. Done. Your miles for the year update every 30 minutes and start fresh every January 1.

Tap the screen for goal & settings. Hold 10 seconds to erase and start over.
