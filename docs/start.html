Next Mile Tracker

Setup takes about 5 minutes. You need your phone and your home WiFi password.

  1. 1
    Plug it in with the USB cable and any phone charger. The screen says Welcome! and shows a square code.
  2. 2
    Connect it to your WiFi. On the tracker's screen, tap your home WiFi, type its password and tap Connect.

    Don't see your WiFi? Tap Look again.

  3. 3
    Connect your watch app. The screen says WiFi connected! and shows a new code. With your phone on your home WiFi, point the camera at it and pick the app your watch uses.
  4. 4
    Done. Your miles for the year show up and update by themselves. Tap the screen any time for settings.

More help: ds3design.github.io/nextmile-tracker
