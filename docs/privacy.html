Next Mile Tracker privacy policy

Next Mile Tracker is a small desk display made by DS3design / Next Mile Apparel that shows the owner's running miles for the year.

What it reads

When you link a watch account (Garmin, Polar, Google Health for Fitbit/Pixel, Apple Health via Shortcut, or Strava), the tracker reads only your workout records: activity type, date, distance and duration. It does not read heart rate, sleep, location tracks, contacts or any other data.

Where it goes

Your data and sign-in tokens are stored only on the tracker itself, on your home network. Nothing is sent to DS3design, Next Mile Apparel or any other server. The linking page on this site only passes the one-time sign-in code from your browser to the tracker on your own WiFi; it does not store or log it.

Removing access

Hold a finger on the tracker's screen for 10 seconds to erase everything on it. You can also revoke access at any time from your watch account's connected-apps settings (for Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions).
